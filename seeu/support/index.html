Support & Contact

Help with SeeU, location sharing, accounts, subscriptions and privacy.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Contact SeeU support

For SeeU technical assistance, privacy requests, misuse reports or account help, contact flirtyquiz.contact@gmail.com. Developer: Laurențiu Iancu, Quiz Fans Studio, Romania.

Email SeeU support

Helpful subjects: “SeeU support”, “SeeU privacy request”, “SeeU safety report”, or “SeeU account deletion request”. Please do not send passwords, one-time codes or another person's location history.

Location not updating

Check that Android Location is switched on, SeeU has the relevant permission, GPS tracking was intentionally started and Circle-specific sharing is enabled. Battery optimizations, a weak signal and background restrictions can delay position updates and alerts. Grant only the permissions necessary for the features you choose.

Circles, privacy and account control

For Circle privacy, open Profile → Privacy & Permissions or the relevant Circle's sharing controls. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions. To request removal of an account without access to the app, see Account & Data Deletion.

Subscriptions

If a Google Play subscription is active, manage it in Google Play. Cancelling a subscription and requesting SeeU account deletion are separate actions. Contact support about unexpected billing or account-access problems without sharing payment-card credentials or authentication secrets.

Safety and misuse

Report covert tracking, stalking, harassment or unauthorized sharing. Include the minimum context required for investigation. SeeU is not an emergency response or rescue service. Contact local emergency services directly if anyone is in immediate danger.
