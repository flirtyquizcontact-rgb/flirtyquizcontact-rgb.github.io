Location not updating?

Check that Android Location is enabled, the app has permission, Start GPS was deliberately selected and location sharing is enabled for the correct Circle. On devices with aggressive power saving, background tracking and activity detection can be delayed.

Safety and privacy choices

Open Profile → Privacy & Permissions to review your location permissions and Circle-specific sharing. Use Profile → Legal & Privacy to read the current policy inside the app. SeeU is not an emergency response service.

Reporting a privacy concern

Describe the relevant problem without sending passwords, authentication codes or someone else's private location records. For account deletion, visit Account & Data Deletion.
