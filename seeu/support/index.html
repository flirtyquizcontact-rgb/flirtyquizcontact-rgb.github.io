Pre-release documentation
BETA SERVICE NOTICE · 8 November 2026: Deletion request intake is available; complete server-side erasure, retention, age safeguards and active paid offers still require verification before public launch. This notice does not restrict your statutory rights.

Contact for support and privacy

Email flirtyquiz.contact@gmail.com. Suggested subjects include SeeU support, SeeU privacy request, SeeU safety report or SeeU account deletion request. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or another person's private GPS history.

Location not updating?

Check that Android Location and the applicable permissions are enabled, that tracking was deliberately started and that location sharing was enabled for the relevant Circle. Background power limits and network conditions may cause delays.

Account deletion and data requests

For deletion even after uninstalling, use the external path on Account & Data Deletion. Account deletion is a request requiring processing; the available beta backend has not yet been verified for complete automatic erasure. You can also request access, correction or another GDPR right via the same contact email.

Safety

SeeU is not an emergency response service and cannot guarantee location accuracy or notification delivery. In immediate danger contact the appropriate local emergency service.
