Privacy Policy

How the SeeU beta processes account, Circle, location and safety information.

Pre-release documentation
BETA NOTICE · 8 November 2026: Full account erasure, per-category data retention, age safeguards, supplier disclosures and activated paid offers still require operational verification. This does not limit your statutory rights.

Controller and contact

SeeU is provided under the public developer name Quiz Fans Studio by Laurențiu Iancu, an independent developer established in Romania. For the SeeU processing described here, this individual is the data controller, not a separately incorporated studio. Privacy contact: flirtyquiz.contact@gmail.com. Mandatory complete provider identification and contact/address details remain under final legal review before commercial release.

Information and purposes

Depending on features and your choices, SeeU may process authentication identifiers, email/name, optional profile data/photos, Circle memberships and invitations, per-Circle permissions, precise coordinates and accuracy/timestamps, speed/movement and battery indicators, recent positions and routes, places, messages, check-ins, SOS or other safety records, push tokens, and support requests. Google Play purchase and entitlement metadata may be involved only where billing is enabled. These data support requested account, Circle, communication, safety, troubleshooting and security functions. A technical inventory of all SDK/log data remains open.

GDPR lawful bases and separate choices

Lawful bases must be assigned to each operation: contractual necessity may apply to core requested services; consent where legally required for optional sharing; appropriately assessed legitimate interests for security; and legal obligations for specific records. Accepting Terms, granting Android location permission and consenting to share with a specific Circle are different actions. A Circle invitation is not automatic authorization to reveal location or history. Withdrawal of optional sharing affects future processing, not previously lawful processing.

Precise location and background use

When users deliberately start tracking and authorize Android permissions, precise location may be updated while SeeU runs in the background or is not visible. Circle members see information only according to sharing settings implemented for that Circle. Users can stop tracking, revoke individual Circle visibility or Android permission. This does not automatically erase previously stored records or copies others have already viewed. Location may reveal sensitive visits such as home, school or work. The prominent in-app background-location disclosure and permission sequence require a separate release test.

Recipients and Google services

Approved Circle participants may receive profile, location, history, message and safety information users choose to make available. The reviewed implementation uses or references Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Realtime Database, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Maps and Google Play services. Provider roles, region, international transfers, subprocessors and applicable processing terms require production verification. We do not claim exclusive EU storage.

Retention and account erasure

Retention must be specified separately for locations, history/archive, latest positions, places, chat, avatars, account/Circle records, safety records, purchase metadata, support emails, logs and backups. End-to-end physical expiry/erasure has not been verified; no universal deletion deadline is claimed. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete cloud information. The reviewed account-deletion function seeuAccountDeletionRequest records a request but has not been shown to remove all associated Firebase data. See Account & Data Deletion.

Rights and supervisory authority

Under applicable law you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, objection and withdrawal of consent where relevant. Send requests to flirtyquiz.contact@gmail.com. Secure, proportionate identity verification may be requested; never send passwords or sign-in codes. GDPR requests are normally answered within one month, subject to lawful extensions. You may complain to ANSPDCP or another competent supervisory authority.

Security, families and limitations

SeeU uses account-based access mechanisms; final review is still needed for production security rules, retention jobs, backups and incident handling. No app guarantees flawless GPS or message delivery; SeeU is not an emergency dispatch service. The service is not designed specifically for young children. An intended age 13+ does not eliminate child-protection, parental-authorization or Google Play obligations in jurisdictions where they apply; age and guardian safeguards have not yet been validated.

Subscriptions and changes

Free, Plus and Pro are the planned tiers. Actual payments, trials and promotions depend on activated Google Play offers. Any purchase-related personal data must be incorporated in the verified data inventory and retention table. This beta notice is dated 8 November 2026; it will be revised when the processing inventory, retention and operational tests are complete.
