Privacy Policy

How the SeeU beta processes account, Circle, location and safety information.

Pre-release documentation
BETA SERVICE NOTICE: This document describes the current SeeU test build. Paid subscriptions, verified account-data deletion and final server-side retention controls are not yet operational. They must be completed and tested before the public Google Play release.

1. Publisher and contact

SeeU is developed and operated by Laurențiu Iancu, an independent developer based in Romania who uses Quiz Fans Studio as the public developer name. Contact for privacy questions: flirtyquiz.contact@gmail.com. The data controller is Laurențiu Iancu, an independent developer based in Romania, publishing under the studio name Quiz Fans Studio. Quiz Fans Studio is a public-facing developer name, not a claim that a separate company operates the service.

2. Information used by SeeU

Depending on your choices: Google/Firebase login identifiers, name and optional photograph; Circle memberships, invitation tokens, roles and consent settings; chat messages, reactions, safety requests and check-ins; device notification tokens; location coordinates, accuracy, timestamp, estimated speed, battery level and motion category; optional places and route history; optional contact/social details added to your profile; local settings and support correspondence.

3. Location and permission controls

You must start tracking and grant the appropriate Android permissions. When you enable the tracking foreground service, location may update while SeeU is not on screen. Each Circle has its own location-sharing setting, and optional shared route history requires separate permission. A Circle invitation never automatically enables sharing. You can stop tracking, revoke Circle sharing and change notification or motion permissions.

Live location can disclose sensitive patterns about home, school, work and travel. Never invite or track someone without their knowledge and appropriate permission.

4. Why data is processed

The beta uses data to authenticate you, maintain Circle membership, display locations only as authorized, exchange messages, provide safety and check-in features, remember your preferences and answer support requests. For the current beta, account registration and core Circle operations rely on providing the requested service; optional live location and shared route history are controlled by separate revocable choices; security and service-abuse prevention may rely on legitimate interests where applicable. The legal-basis assessment and retention schedule still require final review before production release. Android location permission by itself is not consent to share with a Circle.

5. People and providers who may receive data

Other approved Circle members may see the profile, status, location and communications you have chosen to share. The app uses Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Realtime Database, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Maps and Android/Google Play services where enabled. The actual database region, cross-border transfers and provider processing arrangements must be checked before publication; EU-only storage is not currently claimed.

6. Retention and removal

The beta's route-history design is based on a limited 48-hour window, but production server-side deletion has not been verified. The latest shared position may be displayed for up to seven days when consent is active; this does not guarantee automatic server deletion after seven days. Retention periods for messages, profiles, shared photos, notification tokens and safety records still require technical verification.

7. Your privacy rights

Subject to the applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection and withdrawal of consent, and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact flirtyquiz.contact@gmail.com. See also the account-deletion page. The account-deletion process is still being implemented and is not represented as complete.

8. Security and service limitations

SeeU uses Android and Firebase security technologies but has not completed its final review of production database rules, data deletion and the reliability of background safety notifications. The app cannot guarantee continuous location coverage, accuracy or emergency notification delivery. It is not an emergency dispatch service.

9. Children and families

SeeU may be used to coordinate family Circles, but any release for minors requires appropriate age handling, guardian-consent rules and a review of Google Play's applicable child-safety policies. No one may secretly track another person using SeeU.

10. Payments and changes

Free, Plus and Pro are planned offerings. In this build, premium billing and secure entitlement verification are not active. If purchases are introduced, this policy will be updated to explain purchase records and verification. This draft is dated 2 November 2026 and will be revised before release.
