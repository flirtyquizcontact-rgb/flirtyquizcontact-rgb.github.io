Privacy Policy

How SeeU processes account, Circle, location, communication and safety information.

Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. Controller and contact

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android app SeeU by Quiz Fans Studio. Its operator and personal-data controller is Laurențiu Iancu, an independent developer based in Romania. Quiz Fans Studio is a public developer name, not a representation of a separate corporation. Privacy and data-protection requests: flirtyquiz.contact@gmail.com.

2. Personal data we process

Depending on the functions you actually use, SeeU may access or process:

  • Account and profile: Firebase/Google authentication identifiers, email, display name, optional photo, profile settings and an account identifier.
  • Circle activity: invitations, members, roles, Circle identifiers, approvals, sharing choices and related administration records.
  • Location and movement: precise coordinates, timestamps, location accuracy, last-known position, route samples, estimated speed, movement mode and battery-related status, when relevant features are active.
  • Communications and safety: chats, reactions, check-ins, SOS or safety alerts and notification delivery tokens, where used.
  • Places and preferences: user-defined places and settings; some places information is stored locally on the device rather than sent to SeeU's backend.
  • Purchases: Google Play subscription status, product identifiers and purchase-verification metadata when a billing transaction is used. We do not ask for your payment-card number in SeeU.
  • Support and technical data: messages you send to support, operational events and security or error records needed to operate and protect the service.

Not every feature or data category is active for every user, and optional sharing remains controlled by the relevant member.

3. Purposes and lawful bases

We use account, membership and messaging information to provide the service you request, generally on the basis of performing the service contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Optional location and route sharing use the permissions and affirmative sharing choices available in SeeU; where consent is the applicable legal basis, it can be withdrawn through the corresponding controls (Article 6(1)(a)). Security, misuse prevention and service integrity may rely on legitimate interests, subject to applicable safeguards and balancing (Article 6(1)(f)). Purchase administration and records needed to satisfy legal duties may rely on contract performance or legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).

Accepting the Terms does not automatically switch on GPS tracking, give an Android runtime permission or approve disclosure of your location to a Circle.

4. Location and background operation

When you start GPS and authorize Android location access, SeeU may collect updated locations while the app is not visible if the applicable background/foreground-service behavior is enabled. Sharing live location with a Circle and optional sharing of route history require their own user controls. A Circle invitation does not activate tracking. You can stop tracking, change Circle sharing, withdraw an optional choice, or revoke Android permissions.

Location can reveal sensitive patterns, such as visits to your home, school or workplace. Do not use SeeU to monitor anyone secretly. Connectivity, device power management and permission changes can interrupt updates.

5. Who can access information

Approved Circle members may receive the information you choose to share with that Circle, potentially including a live or last-known position, history, safety events and messages. Another member may save information already visible to them outside SeeU, which our controls cannot retrieve.

The app uses or integrates Google services such as Firebase Authentication, Firebase Realtime Database, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Maps/Play services and Google Play Billing where applicable. Service providers process technical information as required to deliver their functions. Information is not made public merely because you create an account. Different Google services may process data in different locations; we do not claim exclusive EU storage.

6. Retention

Our retention periods depend on the type of information and the feature:

  • Route samples: the implemented short-history cleanup is designed around approximately 24 hours. Where paid archive access is actually enabled, configured windows are up to 7 days for Plus and up to 30 days for Pro. Scheduled deletion may involve a processing delay; these figures are not a universal retention period for all SeeU data.
  • Live/last-known location: stored for active location features and subject to tracking changes, Circle deletion and applicable cleanup. A previously displayed or saved position may not disappear immediately from every recipient's device.
  • Account, Circle and content records: held while needed to provide active account functions. The authenticated in-app erasure procedure removes supported account-linked records from the active database during processing, subject to legal exceptions and other users' rights.
  • Purchase, support, security and backup records: retained only where needed for the applicable purpose, lawful requirements, security or technical backup lifecycle. Such records may have different retention criteria.

The exact end-to-end timing for every backend and backup category has not been independently confirmed. If a particular record is retained following an erasure request, we will explain the applicable reason where legally required.

7. Deletion and user rights

To delete your SeeU account, use Profile → Legal & Privacy → Account deletion while signed in, or follow the external account and data deletion instructions without signing in. In SeeU 1.12.3 and later, confirming in-app deletion starts an authenticated server-side operation immediately, without an imposed waiting period or manual approval. The app reports success only after a successful server response. External email requests may require proportionate ownership verification. Uninstalling SeeU or signing out does not delete your cloud account.

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, object to particular processing and withdraw consent where relevant. Email flirtyquiz.contact@gmail.com. We may take proportionate steps to verify account ownership. GDPR requests are generally answered within one month, with lawful exceptions or extensions. You can complain to the Romanian supervisory authority (ANSPDCP) or another competent data-protection authority.

8. Security and international processing

SeeU uses authenticated accounts and database access controls, with service-side functions for selected operations. No system offers absolute security. The exact infrastructure, provider terms and any applicable international-transfer safeguards depend on the Google service and deployment. Contact us if you need more information about the services handling your information.

9. Family use and minors

SeeU is for transparent coordination among consenting family members and friends, not hidden surveillance. A parent or guardian must be involved where applicable laws require their authorization for a child's data processing or use of the service. A guardian's involvement does not authorize unlawful or covert monitoring. Age-related requirements differ by jurisdiction and processing purpose.

10. Subscriptions, changes and contact

Free, Plus and Pro features depend on the functions activated in the app. When purchases are offered, Google Play displays the current prices, trial eligibility, renewal and cancellation terms before purchase. Cancelling a subscription and requesting account deletion are separate processes.

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app, service providers or legal requirements, using an updated date and any other notice required by law. Last updated: 9 November 2026. Contact: flirtyquiz.contact@gmail.com.
