Control stays with each member

An invitation to a Circle is not permission to reveal someone's location. Every member controls whether GPS is started and whether a particular Circle may see their location or route history.

About the project

SeeU is a family-and-friends location and safety coordination app developed by Laurențiu Iancu, an independent developer in Romania, under the public name Quiz Fans Studio. The published beta does not currently sell Plus or Pro subscriptions. Service capabilities and privacy protections must pass a final audit before public launch.
