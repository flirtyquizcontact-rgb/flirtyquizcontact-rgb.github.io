Your location, your choice

SeeU helps people coordinate within invitation-based Circles. Granting Android location permission or joining a Circle does not by itself give other members permission to see your position. Live tracking and sharing with individual Circles are separate choices that you can change.

Location and safety alerts can be delayed or unavailable; SeeU is not an emergency response service.

Account control

In SeeU 1.12.3 and later, a signed-in user can confirm account deletion in the app. The server starts processing the deletion immediately, without a fixed waiting period; the app reports success only after the server confirms completion. If you cannot sign in or have uninstalled SeeU, you can still request account deletion on the web. Any applicable retention exceptions are explained in our Privacy Policy.
