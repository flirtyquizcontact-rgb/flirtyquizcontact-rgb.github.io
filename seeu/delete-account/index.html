Account & Data Deletion

How to contact us about account removal, and what remains before public launch.

Pre-release documentation
BETA SERVICE NOTICE · 8 November 2026: Deletion request intake is available; complete server-side erasure, retention, age safeguards and active paid offers still require verification before public launch. This notice does not restrict your statutory rights.

How to request deletion

Inside SeeU, signed-in users can initiate an account-deletion request from the Profile / Legal & Privacy area where available in their build. The reviewed backend receives the request as a pending action, not a guarantee of instant or completed deletion.

External request without reinstalling

Send an email to flirtyquiz.contact@gmail.com with subject SeeU account deletion request. State the account email or relevant identifier and clearly ask to delete the SeeU account and associated data. We may need to verify account ownership securely. Never send passwords, one-time codes, private keys or other members' location records.

Data included in the review

The intended erasure scope includes the Firebase Authentication identity for SeeU, profile information and avatar, Circle memberships/roles and invites, consent records, GPS positions and history/archive, saved places, notification tokens, attributable chat and safety records, connected storage and backups. It must be checked against the real production schema. Rights of other members and any lawful, documented retention exceptions must be respected.

What is and is not verified

The function seeuAccountDeletionRequest records the request for operator processing; full deletion across Authentication, Realtime Database, storage and backups has not yet passed an end-to-end test. We therefore do not claim that automated erasure is already complete. You may request a status update or exercise GDPR rights via the email above. Applicable response deadlines continue to apply. Removing a SeeU Firebase identity does not delete your personal Google account.

Sharing and subscriptions

To stop future location sharing without deleting the account, stop tracking and adjust each Circle's sharing permissions. This does not necessarily remove existing cloud records or copies others already viewed. If you hold an active subscription, manage it separately in Google Play Subscriptions; deleting the app or SeeU account does not necessarily cancel billing.
