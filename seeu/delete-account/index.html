Account & Data Deletion

How to contact us about account removal, and what remains before public launch.

Pre-release documentation
BETA SERVICE NOTICE: This document describes the current SeeU test build. Paid subscriptions, verified account-data deletion and final server-side retention controls are not yet operational. They must be completed and tested before the public Google Play release.

SeeU-specific account deletion request

This page applies specifically to SeeU, which uses Firebase/Google sign-in and cloud data. The general deletion documentation for other Quiz Fans Studio apps, many of which are offline, does not replace this SeeU procedure. View the studio-wide deletion information for other apps.

For this pre-release version, contact the developer at flirtyquiz.contact@gmail.com with the subject SeeU account deletion request. Identify the Google account used for SeeU, but never send your password. The developer may request secure verification of account ownership. You may request deletion even if you have already uninstalled SeeU. Do not include someone else's GPS history in the email.

Email a deletion request

What data must be covered

The final removal process must account for Firebase Authentication identities; Circle memberships and ownership; consents and invitation data; shared location, last-known positions and route history; optional profile photos/details; tokens, chat and safety records where attributable to the person, with safeguards for other Circle members' rights.

Current technical status

The beta has not yet passed an end-to-end account deletion test. An email request is a contact method, not proof that automated or complete removal is available. We are not claiming compliance with the Google Play account-deletion requirement yet.

Before Google Play publication

A discoverable in-app deletion control, an external deletion-request route, secure identity checks, backend data cleanup, a verifiable completion process and documented lawful retention exceptions must be implemented and tested. Users will be informed of the final process when it is live.
