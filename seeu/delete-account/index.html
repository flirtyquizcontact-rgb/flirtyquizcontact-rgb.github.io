Account & Data Deletion

Request removal of a SeeU account whether or not you can sign in.

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Request deletion of your SeeU account

This page is the external account-deletion route for SeeU by Quiz Fans Studio. You can submit a request even if SeeU is uninstalled or you cannot sign in.

Request account deletion by email

Email flirtyquiz.contact@gmail.com with the subject SeeU account deletion request. Tell us which SeeU account you want removed (for example, the email address associated with it) and whether you can still sign in. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, purchase tokens, private GPS records or someone else's information. We may verify your account ownership using a proportionate method before deletion.

Request deletion from within SeeU

If you can sign in, open Profile → Legal & Privacy → Account deletion and review the confirmation before deleting. In SeeU 1.12.3 and later, confirming starts authenticated server-side deletion immediately, without a fixed waiting period or manual approval. The app displays success only after the server confirms completion. If an error occurs, deletion has not been confirmed; you may retry or contact support. If you cannot sign in or have uninstalled SeeU, use the email request route above.

What is deleted

The in-app server operation is designed to remove your SeeU authentication identity, profile, Circle participation and invitations, location and last-known positions, route history and paid archive records attributable to you, device push tokens, applicable chats and safety records, and other account-linked data in SeeU's active database, subject to legitimate legal retention exceptions and other users' rights.

If you own a Circle, deleting your account also deletes that Circle and its shared content, including content contributed by other members. SeeU presents this consequence before confirmation. Information another member previously saved outside SeeU, device-local files and provider backups are not necessarily removed by the same server operation. You may remove private device-only information by clearing app data or uninstalling SeeU.

How the request is processed

The signed-in app submits one authenticated deletion operation. There is no imposed 70-minute waiting period or manual approval for this in-app path. A success message means the server has confirmed the operation; a failed request or a pending status is not proof that deletion completed. Google Play subscriptions are managed separately from SeeU account deletion. External email requests require proportionate verification and individual processing.

We aim to process verified requests without undue delay and respond to rights requests within applicable statutory deadlines, generally one month under GDPR, subject to permitted extensions and exceptions. We do not claim an unverified universal deletion deadline for all provider backups and third-party copies.

What may be retained

Limited records may be retained only where a valid legal obligation, security/fraud-prevention reason, the rights of another person, or an applicable backup lifecycle justifies retention. Retention depends on the record and its purpose; we do not claim an unverified universal deletion deadline for backup or third-party records. Our Privacy Policy explains the categories and retention criteria.

Google Play purchase records and subscription controls are operated separately by Google. To stop renewal, manage the subscription in Google Play Subscriptions. Account deletion and cancellation are separate actions; contact support if an active subscription creates a problem completing your request.

Delete location sharing without deleting the account

You can stop GPS, revoke Android location permission, disable sharing with an individual Circle or leave a Circle. These are not substitutes for requesting full account deletion and may not erase information other members have already seen or saved. For full account deletion, use the request button above.

Help and privacy rights

Support and privacy email: flirtyquiz.contact@gmail.com. You may also request access, correction or erasure of particular personal data under applicable law. Please include only the minimum information needed to identify your request.
