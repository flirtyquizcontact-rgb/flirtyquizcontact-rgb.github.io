Account & Data Deletion

Request removal of a SeeU account whether or not you can sign in.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Request deletion of your SeeU account

This page is the external account-deletion route for SeeU by Quiz Fans Studio. You can submit a request even if SeeU is uninstalled or you cannot sign in.

Request account deletion by email

Email flirtyquiz.contact@gmail.com with the subject SeeU account deletion request. Tell us which SeeU account you want removed (for example, the email address associated with it) and whether you can still sign in. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, purchase tokens, private GPS records or someone else's information. We may verify your account ownership using a proportionate method before deletion.

Request deletion from within SeeU

If you can sign in, open Profile → Legal & Privacy → Account deletion, review the information and submit your request. You can check the request status in the same area while the feature remains available. This submits a request for review; it does not immediately delete all cloud data. You do not have to reinstall the app to use the external email route.

What is deleted

When the request has been verified and processed, the deletion procedure is intended to cover your SeeU account identity, profile, your Circle participation and invitations, location and last-known positions, route history and any paid archive data attributable to you, device push tokens, applicable chats, safety records and other account-linked SeeU information, subject to another user's rights and legitimate retention exceptions.

Some shared Circle material may involve other users and must be assessed carefully. Private device-only information can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling SeeU; this is separate from the server-side account-deletion request.

How the request is processed

We review account ownership and any dependencies such as shared Circle ownership before completing data removal. Where necessary, we may ask you to transfer Circle ownership or contact support about an active Google Play subscription. Submission, an account suspension or a status of pending review is not proof that deletion has completed. You may contact us to ask about progress.

We aim to process verified requests without undue delay and respond to rights requests within applicable statutory deadlines, generally one month under GDPR, subject to permitted extensions and exceptions. We will not describe the account as erased until the relevant deletion process has actually completed.

What may be retained

Limited records may be retained only where a valid legal obligation, security/fraud-prevention reason, the rights of another person, or an applicable backup lifecycle justifies retention. Retention depends on the record and its purpose; we do not claim an unverified universal deletion deadline for backup or third-party records. Our Privacy Policy explains the categories and retention criteria.

Google Play purchase records and subscription controls are operated separately by Google. To stop renewal, manage the subscription in Google Play Subscriptions. Account deletion and cancellation are separate actions; contact support if an active subscription creates a problem completing your request.

Delete location sharing without deleting the account

You can stop GPS, revoke Android location permission, disable sharing with an individual Circle or leave a Circle. These are not substitutes for requesting full account deletion and may not erase information other members have already seen or saved. For full account deletion, use the request button above.

Help and privacy rights

Support and privacy email: flirtyquiz.contact@gmail.com. You may also request access, correction or erasure of particular personal data under applicable law. Please include only the minimum information needed to identify your request.
