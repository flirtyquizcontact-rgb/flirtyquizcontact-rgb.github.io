1. Who we are and scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Quiz Fans Studio (developer Iancu Laurentiu, Romania, referred to as “we”) handles information associated with the Android game Block Puzzle Game: Vortex (the “Game”). It covers the Game, not unrelated apps or third-party websites.

Quick summary: Gameplay progress and preferences may be kept on your device. The Game contains advertising and its Google Play Data safety listing discloses collection and sharing of app activity, app information and performance, and device or other identifiers. Third-party providers may process information for advertising, diagnostics and supported game services. You can contact us about your privacy rights and deletion requests.

2. Categories of information

2.1 Gameplay and device-local data

To support gameplay, the Game may store settings, music and sound preferences, haptics, tutorial state, board/session state, scores, progression, levels, missions, achievements, daily rewards, virtual crystals, cosmetics or skins, events and other in-game rewards locally. Saved progress is not necessarily synchronized across devices.

2.2 Device, activity and technical data

The public Google Play Data safety declaration identifies app activity, app information and performance, and device or other identifiers as data categories that may be collected and shared. Depending on enabled services, this can include usage or interaction events, crash and diagnostic information, app/OS version, device characteristics, advertising identifiers and network information such as IP address.

2.3 Optional platform services

If you use Google Play Games functionality, the platform may process a player identifier, display name, profile image, achievements, scores and leaderboard activity. Where notifications are supported and enabled, a device messaging token or delivery diagnostics may be processed.

Core puzzle gameplay does not require a separate account created directly with Quiz Fans Studio. The Game is not designed to request your contacts, microphone recordings, precise location or private files for its core puzzle mechanics.

3. Third-party services

Depending on the installed version and activated features, the Game's previously published technical disclosures identify the following Google services:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): serving and measuring ads, processing ad requests and interactions, preventing fraud.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): obtaining and storing advertising consent and privacy choices where required.
  • Firebase Analytics: events and usage measurements for understanding Game performance and interactions, when enabled.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash logs and device/app diagnostics used to troubleshoot stability, when enabled.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: notification tokens and delivery information if push notifications are implemented and used.
  • Firebase Remote Config: delivering remote feature or configuration settings, if enabled.
  • Google Play Games Services: achievements, leaderboards and profile-related data when used.

Actual processing depends on which SDKs and features are present and active in the installed release. Google processes information under its own terms and policies; see the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Why we process data

  • Provide gameplay, local settings, resume functionality, daily challenges, rewards and score progression.
  • Support optional achievements, leaderboards and notifications.
  • Deliver and measure advertising, manage user consent and prevent ad fraud.
  • Understand usage, investigate crashes, maintain security and improve quality, where analytics/diagnostics are enabled.
  • Respond to privacy requests, prevent misuse and comply with applicable legal obligations.

EEA/UK legal bases: depending on the activity, processing may rely on performance of the requested service, legitimate interests such as security and diagnostics (subject to legal balancing requirements), consent for processing that legally requires it, or legal obligations. Consent can be withdrawn where consent is the legal basis; this does not automatically affect earlier lawful processing.

5. Advertising and privacy choices

The Game contains ads, which may include rewarded ads or interstitial ads, depending on availability and the installed release. Advertising providers may process ad identifiers, device information, network signals and ad interactions for delivery, measurement, frequency controls and fraud prevention.

Where required, the Game may present a Google UMP consent message or privacy-options entry point. You can also review relevant choices in the Game if available and through Android / Google privacy settings. Rejecting optional personalized-ad processing does not necessarily remove all advertising.

6. Recipients and international processing

Information may be transmitted to Google's advertising, Firebase and Play Games infrastructure when the corresponding service is used. These providers may process data on systems outside your country, subject to the provider's applicable safeguards. We may also disclose limited information where legally required or to protect the Game from fraud and security abuse.

We do not independently sell personal data as a separate data-broker activity. Advertising identifiers and related signals may nonetheless be shared with advertising providers as disclosed above and in Google Play Data safety.

7. Storage, retention and deletion requests

Local Game data: gameplay state and settings may remain on your device until cleared, overwritten, or removed through Android app storage controls or uninstalling the Game. Deleting local data may cause permanent loss of unsynchronized progress.

Provider-held data: retention for advertising, analytics, diagnostics and Google Play Games information depends on the applicable provider, processing purpose and configured retention periods. Uninstalling the Game does not necessarily erase records already held by third-party services.

Privacy and deletion requests: email flirtyquiz.contact@gmail.com, identify the Game and explain your access, correction or deletion request. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information. We will assess requests under applicable law and explain if data is held or controlled by a provider whose separate controls you must use. No in-game Quiz Fans Studio account is required for core gameplay.

8. Your rights and controls

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, object to certain processing, withdraw consent and complain to a competent data protection authority. These rights are subject to applicable conditions and exceptions.

  • Clear Game data or uninstall through Android settings to manage locally stored information.
  • Manage notification permissions through Android settings.
  • Use available in-Game advertising/privacy settings or device and Google controls.
  • Manage Google Play Games information through the relevant Google account controls.
  • Contact us for a privacy request at the email above.

9. Children and families

The Game is presented as a general-audience puzzle game, not as a children-only service. If you are a parent or guardian concerned about information processed in connection with a child, contact us at the address above. Age-related protections and advertising obligations vary by region and platform.

10. Data security

We seek to use reasonable technical and organizational security safeguards appropriate to the Game and its providers. Google Play currently declares data encrypted in transit. No method of processing or storage is completely risk-free.

11. Updates and contact

We may revise this Policy to reflect changes in the Game, its providers or legal requirements. The effective date at the top identifies the published version. For any questions about the Game or your privacy, contact flirtyquiz.contact@gmail.com.

See also our Terms of Service and the Game's Google Play listing and Data safety information.
